Friday, October 16

Hollow-Queen: Cocktail Drag Spook-tacular

Doors 6pm · Show 7pm

Tickets $20 · 18+

Presented with
State Queens

Titan Hollow and State Queens are teaming up to raise the dead (and the roof). The divas of State Queens bring their most spellbinding, spine-chilling numbers to the Titan Hollow stage, with themed cocktails made with Mad McIntosh Cider. Expect eerie elegance, killer looks, and performances dripping with Halloween magic. Dress to impress: costumes are welcomed and encouraged.
